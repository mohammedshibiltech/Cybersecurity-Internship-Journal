## Vulnerability Assessment (VA)

A systematic process used to identify, classify, and prioritize vulnerabilities in systems and applications.

### Characteristics

- Broad coverage
- Automated scanning
- Risk identification
- Prioritization of findings

---

## Penetration Testing (PT)

An authorized simulation of an attack to determine the real-world impact of vulnerabilities.

### Characteristics

- Exploitation-focused
- Demonstrates attack paths
- Validates vulnerabilities
- Provides proof of impact

---

## VA vs PT

| Vulnerability Assessment | Penetration Testing |
|-------------------------|--------------------|
| Identifies weaknesses | Exploits weaknesses |
| Broad coverage | Deep testing |
| Mostly automated | Mostly manual |
| Risk-focused | Impact-focused |
